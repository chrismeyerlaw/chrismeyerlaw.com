About Chris Meyer

I am an attorney with a diverse professional background that informs a practical, client-centered approach to legal services. I earned my undergraduate degree from Miami University in Oxford, where I studied Bioengineering with a concentration in Bioinformatics. I earned my Juris Doctorate from Chase Law School at Northern Kentucky.

Before entering the legal field, I worked across a range of industries, including clinical research, healthcare, public service with the Cincinnati Police, and as a software developer in a large corporate environment. My background in software development has allowed me to build meaningful connections within the legal community, including my work with Lawyers With Purpose, where I contribute technical expertise while gaining access to a strong network and estate planning resources.

As an attorney, I have gained experience in family law and estate planning through my work with a local law firm. I have also worked with the Ohio Justice and Policy Center and have experience in the patent space, giving me a broad perspective across multiple areas of law. I volunteer at the Family Law Clinic in Hamilton County and run a Law Club at my High School The School for Creative and Performing Arts.

In addition to my legal work, I have spent over a decade as a senior caregiver. Through that experience, I have witnessed firsthand both the challenges and the profound importance of thoughtful estate planning. Many of the families I have worked with have become like family to me, and those experiences continue to shape the way I approach my practice.

Above all, I am a husband and a father. Family is central to everything I do, and it is the lens through which I approach my work with clients. I am a lifelong learner, always seeking to grow and better serve the people who place their trust in me.

Outside of my professional life, I am an ultramarathon runner. I regularly pace runners at the Flying Pig Marathon and am the youngest ten-time finisher of the Stone Steps 50K.
